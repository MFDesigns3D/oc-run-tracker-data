Pay breakdown (like your paystub)
Pay rate & overtime
Enter your regular hourly rate (3 decimals, like 37.910). Everything else is worked out from it. When you get a raise, tap Add new rate and set the date it starts. Older pay periods keep the old rate.
How pay is worked out (each shift counts on the day it starts):
• Overtime / stat worked: 1.5×
• CLC break: 30 min each, part of your shift time, paid at regular rate. Doesn't count toward OT (a 10:47 shift with 2 CLC = 9:47 work, pays 10:47). Set them per run in My work (max 2 a day). General spare: pick how many on the Hours tab. Tick Add CLC on a day for an extra one (e.g. when you extend)
• Day with a loose piece (booked for the whole booking): 8 h regular, the rest is OT that day
• Extended (extra trips picked up at the end of a shift): counts toward the 80 h only, no daily OT
• Booking clerk: +$0.50/h premium for clerk hours, and your booked bus work is paid as usual. Extra session (double), or a clerk shift on a weekday off: OT + the premium, not toward the 80
• Stat: 8 h regular, plus 1.5× for any hours worked
• Sick: 90%, 8 h (or the full day if it's 8:54 or more)
• Vacation: 8 h regular
• Sunday: +25% on the whole shift, or +50% if it finishes after 23:30
• General spare, standby: paid from standby start until your bus starts, up to 2 h. If the work was picked after 2 h on standby, all of it is paid. No work = 8 h. Standby days are topped up to 8 h.
• General spare, phone call (9:30 and later): 30 min Callup. Doesn't count toward OT.
• All general spare for the whole pay period: OT daily after 8 h. Any booked shift in the period (booked spare, regular run, stat included): OT after 80 h.

Overtime

Days marked Overtime are all OT. The 80 h counts only hours you actually work (regular + stat worked). Stat pay, CLC breaks, sick, vacation and Sunday premium don't count.
If you edit a day so it's longer than your booked work, the extra is OT.

Booking

Your regular work applies between these dates. Leave Finish blank if you're not sure yet.

2-week rotation

Add each piece you work: run, the time you take over (Start), and the time you finish (24 h, just type 1010). Leave Finish blank to use the end of the run. Pick more days to repeat it. For a general spare day, tap General spare and type your spare time. Booked spare shifts go in as normal runs.

Booking clerk relief

Dates you're off the bus working as booking clerk (Monday to Friday only; weekends follow your normal booking). Your booked work is still paid; days with booked runs get your usual clerk hours. Change a single day or add an extra session on the Hours tab.

Holidays & special days

On these dates, this replaces your rotation. Pick Off, Regular (your usual work that day) or Custom (add runs by hand). Tick Stat for a stat holiday.
